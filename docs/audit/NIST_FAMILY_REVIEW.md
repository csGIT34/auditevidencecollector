# All-family NIST review

Reviewed all 20 family scopes in the pinned SP 800-53/53A catalog. Controls below are **candidates for tailoring**, not a selected baseline. The machine-readable index contains each referenced control title, statement, parameters and assessment methods. The remaining catalog controls/enhancements are not declared irrelevant: assessors must select or justify them from the actual baseline, system, threats and legal obligations. Withdrawn controls are not proposed.

Every family policy/procedure control (and PM-1 program plan) needs an organization/common-control owner, document approval, dissemination and review evidence. Resource-local N/A never excludes that governance responsibility. A conditional exclusion must record the exact control/scope, factual rationale, responsible approver, evidence and review date.

Public provider documentation supplies feature context; obtain authorized assurance reports and evaluate scope/period/exceptions before inheriting operational controls. Do not attempt to discover provider facilities, people or internal systems through customer resource APIs.
